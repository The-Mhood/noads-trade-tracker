## Ratified direction and current pre-POC authorization (owner, 2026-10-10)

- Broker-side pending orders preferred. Risk settings remain live before trigger, but actual fill can use last broker-confirmed volume. Record actual exposure and deviation; **no replacement until authoritative final cancellation and sufficiently complete order/position/deal history prove zero fills**. Acknowledgement/ID/empty list/time is not finality; UNKNOWN triggers a durable scope-aware submission fence: setup-only requires objective proof that account exposure, shared margin, account state and calculations are unaffected; otherwise block the affected account. Shared service failure blocks dependent accounts, not independent reliable ones. Bounded reconciliation escalates without converting UNKNOWN to success; numerical bounds require provider verification.
- No automatic replacement after any partial fill. Automatic residual cancellation is **not approved** pending validation. Track filled/residual volume and actual risk separately; unknown risk is not zero. Never automatically amend/reduce/close/offset unexpected exposure.
- For Phase 1, every active pending order and resulting position must have **broker-confirmed SL/TP** before an account resumes. Owner acknowledgement does not override mismatched/unverified protection. Authoritatively zero exposure makes protection N/A. Full exposure reconciliation and a *separate owner authorization* are required to resume; clear stale intent, do not replay a signal or submit a trade when unblocking. Late contradictory evidence re-blocks.
- Quote age is decision time minus source quote timestamp; validate broker and conversion inputs, fail closed if stale/missing/inconsistent. Exact age, clock semantics, conversion path, fallback and reconciliation thresholds remain open. POC-1 stays OPEN; proposed **$25 total** future-work ceiling is not spend permission and cannot be assumed enforceable.
- Current authorization (2026-10-10) permits authoritative document edits, public-source research, offline harness safeguards/tests, a non-executable costed plan and eligible PR cleanup. It does not permit MetaApi calls, billable resources, POC execution or Phase 1 product code. POC-1 pass must be evidenced and subsequently confirmed by the owner before Phase 1 starts, absent separate explicit changed authorization.
