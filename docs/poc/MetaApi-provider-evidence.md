## Additional public-source research: entry capability (2026-10-10)

The official [MetaApi trade command model](https://metaapi.cloud/docs/client/models/metatraderTrade/) lists eight BUY/SELL market, limit, stop and stop-limit `actionType`s plus order modify/cancel and position modify/close. Stop-limit requests require `openPrice` **and** `stopLimitPrice` (the command model documents the latter as a string); [order readback](https://metaapi.cloud/docs/client/models/metatraderOrder/) describes `stopLimitPrice` as a number. The [symbol specification](https://metaapi.cloud/docs/client/models/metatraderSymbolSpecification/) documents `allowedOrderTypes`, `tradeMode`, sessions, `stopsLevel`, `freezeLevel`, fill/expiry, lot limits and currencies. These are documented **fields/capabilities**, not proof that the designated broker accepts every type, that modification is safe at a particular price, or that REST history is final. The [deals-by-time endpoint](https://metaapi.cloud/docs/client/restApi/api/retrieveHistoricalData/readDealsByTimeRange/) uses a half-open time range with offset/limit pagination; [completed orders by ticket](https://metaapi.cloud/docs/client/restApi/api/retrieveHistoricalData/readHistoryOrdersByTicket/) returns an array. No MetaApi account or trade endpoint was called for this research.
